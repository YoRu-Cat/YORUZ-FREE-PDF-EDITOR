Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no cookies. Your file is uploaded only so it can be edited, nobody looks at it, and it is deleted automatically after a few hours of inactivity, or right away when you close it.

What we collect

  • The files you open. Editing happens on our server, so the file you choose is uploaded to it.
  • Basic server logs. Like almost every website, the server and our hosting provider record technical request logs: IP address, time, and the address that was requested. These logs never contain the contents of your files.

That's it. We don't ask for your name, email or any account, and we don't use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies.

How your files are handled

  • Files travel to the server over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • While you edit, the server keeps a working copy of your document plus earlier versions so Undo works.
  • Each document is identified only by a long random ID that is kept in your own browser. It isn't put in links you share, and nobody else is given it.
  • Your files are processed automatically by software. No person looks at them, and they are never sold, shared, published or used to train AI.
  • When you click Download, the PDF is generated on the fly and sent to you. No extra copy is kept.

When your files are deleted

  • Right away, when you click the ✕ Close button in the editor's top bar.
  • Right away, when you open or create another document in the same browser tab (the previous one is deleted).
  • Automatically, after a few hours without any activity, if you just close the tab.
  • On our free hosting plan, a server restart also wipes all stored documents.

Deleted files and their edit history can't be recovered, so download your work before you leave.

Passwords

If you open a password-protected PDF, the password is used once to unlock it and is not stored. The working copy on the server is kept unencrypted while you edit it, and it is deleted as described above. A password you set when downloading is used only to encrypt that download and is not stored.

Stored in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device, for:

  • the ID of the document you're editing, so a page refresh doesn't lose your work;
  • your tool settings (font, colours, line width…);
  • your signature, only if you tick Remember in the signature window. It is sent to the server only when you place it in a document.

You can clear all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site runs on Render, which provides the servers and may keep its own request logs under its privacy policy.

Very sensitive documents

Because editing happens on our server, please don't upload anything you aren't comfortable sending over the internet, even temporarily. When you're done with a sensitive file, use the ✕ Close button so it is deleted immediately.

Limits

Uploads are limited to the configured size per file. The service is provided free and as-is, with no guarantee of availability.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
